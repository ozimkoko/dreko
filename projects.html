Churchill, Innisfil

One house, rebuilt as four homes

Complete

Purchased as a single-family house, the property was converted by Dreko in 2024 into three self-contained residences, each finished with hardwood floors, tiled baths and its own carpentry details.

Between September 2025 and March 2026 we added a detached garden suite at the rear of the lot, built with our long-standing trade partners. The result is four self-contained homes on what was a single-family lot, and the clearest example of how we work: one team, from the first drawing to the final inspection.

Keswick, on Cook’s Bay

A lakefront cottage, being rebuilt for four seasons

In progress

A cottage twenty feet from a 75-foot shoreline on Cook’s Bay, which Dreko is rebuilding for year-round use as a seasonal rental. The original boathouse has been structurally reinforced and reframed as a kitchen with large windows facing the lake, and a new roof extension now shelters a poured concrete patio at the water’s edge.

The exterior is now complete: a new roof, white vertical siding, new windows and doors, and a gravel court lined with cedars. Inside, the boathouse kitchen sits under a vaulted, wood-panelled ceiling with exposed timber beams, and the living spaces are finished in painted shiplap. Interior finishing is under way.

Aurora

A modern-farmhouse coach house and lap pool

In design

On a deep lot a short walk from Aurora GO, Dreko is designing a two-storey, 24 by 40 ft modern-farmhouse building behind the existing bungalow: a garage and pool room at grade, with a two-bedroom, two-bath suite above. A lap pool runs along the rear garden.

These images are renderings from Dreko’s own 3D site model, built from the zoning drawings so the building, setbacks and neighbouring homes sit where they will in reality. The design is still being refined.
