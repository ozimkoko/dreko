Ontario’s planning rules now allow more homes on a single residential lot. Duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and garden suites are where much of the region’s new housing will come from, and each one is a real development: zoning, servicing, fire separation, budget and schedule.

Dreko brings a developer’s discipline to that scale, and a custom builder’s attention to the homes themselves. We tell you what a lot can become and what it will cost, then build it with trades we have trusted for years.

Design and permits

We work with our partner architects and engineers, walk you through the design in virtual reality, and carry it through municipal approval, so the finished building matches what you signed off.

Walk through it before it is built.

Before construction, Dreko builds a 3D model of your site from the zoning drawings: the new building, the property lines and the neighbouring homes, each where it will stand.

With our partner architects, we then bring that model into virtual reality. You walk the lot and the building itself at full scale: through the front door, up the stairs and into every room, months before the foundation is poured. Changes you would otherwise discover halfway through construction are made on the drawings instead, before they cost you time and money.

A change in the model costs a conversation. On site, it costs weeks and a change order.
